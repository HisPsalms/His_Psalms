About

On this account, we post songs that are meant to praise and worship God. I started this account after listening to the song Essence of Worship on the theme ‘Shangilia’ where the singer mentions Psalm 150, saying ‘Let everything that has breath praise the Lord.’ I came up with the name HisPsalms because the Book of Psalms is full of songs written by King David to God.
